The media of the media library no entry uses, as found by the last check of this blog.

Before deleting
A theme or another plugin may still use a media: open it with its link and look at it first.
Selected lines
Ignore for good the media you keep (the filter "Status" still shows them), forget a line until a next check finds it again, or delete the other ones from the media library for good. Each media is looked for again in the entries before being deleted.
Size
"Missing" means the media is listed in the media library but its file is not on the disk anymore.
Export
The button under the list exports all its lines as a CSV file, for a spreadsheet.
