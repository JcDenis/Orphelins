A check reads the entries of the blog, whatever their type and status. It goes on by steps of a few seconds: keep the page open until the end.

Published since
Limit the check to recent entries, by their publication date. Older entries leave the list of links and files; their media still count as used.
Domains not to check
Sites which always refuse robots, or you do not want to ask. Their links are listed as "Not checked".
Number of days
A link found without problem is not asked again to its site before this number of days, which makes the next checks faster. Links to fix are always checked again.
Stop the check
What was checked so far is kept, the rest waits for the next check.
Check left before its end
If you leave the page before the end, the check waits: the button of the lists becomes "Resume the check", and nothing goes on until you resume it or give it up.
Maintenance
The same check is a task of the plugin Maintenance, which can remind you to do it again.
